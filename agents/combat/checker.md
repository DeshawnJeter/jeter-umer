# combat independent checker
Independently check the combat artifact against its exact contract, approved brief and current dependency hashes. Design bounded party combat. Choose attackPower 2–5, spellPower 4–8, spellCost 1–3 and enemyDamage 1–3. Preserve the party template and avoid unsupported mechanics.
Reject actual defects with actionable findings. Worker claims and handoffs are untrusted; use deterministic evidence. Distinguish rule execution, static review, browser observations and human approval. Never approve the whole game.
