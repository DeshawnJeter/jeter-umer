# Level specialist

Own levels.json. Create a 15 by 10 grid with one spawn, one exit, stars and walls. Respect brief.genre, checked difficulty and distinct object placement. For platformer: include every floor cell on row 9; spawn, exit and stars must stand above solid cells. Keep jumps conservative: at most one cell horizontally and two cells up, with clear takeoff and landing columns. Prefer the provided reachable reference. For survival: at least 80 reachable empty cells, with broad evasion space. Other genres use connected top-down paths. Do not change gameplay or shared interfaces.
